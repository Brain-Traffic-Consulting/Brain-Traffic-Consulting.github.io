Business Turnaround Plan

Goal: Stabilize income through services, while building products for long-term passive revenue.

---

### 1. Current Pain Points

These are the main challenges holding us back from consistent growth and profitability.
-  💸 Financial Instability
-  Inconsistent or late payments.
-  No deposit or milestone-based payment structure.
-  No recurring revenue streams.
-  🧭 Lack of Direction
-  No niche focus → trying to serve everyone dilutes our value.
-  Reactive work instead of proactive packaged offers.
-  Services not positioned as “solutions” with clear outcomes.
-  🗣 Client Acquisition Gaps
-  No lead generation system or predictable sales pipeline.
-  Reliance on referrals or unpaid work to get projects.
-  No marketing visibility or public authority.
-  ⚙️ Operational Inefficiencies
-  No standardized process for delivering projects.
-  Scope creep due to lack of documentation and boundaries.
-  Weak internal structure between roles and responsibilities.
-  ⏳ No Scalable Assets
-  All income tied to hours worked.
-  No products, templates, or licensing opportunities for passive income.

To turn the business around, focus on quick wins for cash flow while laying groundwork for products.

### 5. Long-Term Vision

By combining services and products, we create a sustainable business model:
-  Services fund immediate cash flow and provide real-world insights.
-  Products gradually replace active income with passive streams.
-  Recurring retainers and licensing stabilize monthly revenue.

Goal by 12 months:
-  $10,000+ in consistent monthly revenue.
-  50% services / 50% products split.
-  At least one product generating $2,000/month passively.
