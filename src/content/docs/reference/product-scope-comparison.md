description: Comparison of PoC, MVP, MLP, V1, and full product scopes.
---

This document outlines the different product development stages we use when planning deliverables.  
It helps us align with stakeholders and avoid scope creep.

| **Stage** | **Purpose** | **Scope** | **Timeline** |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|--------------|
| **PoC (Proof of Concept)** | Validate technical feasibility | Disposable code, internal demo only | Days → 2 weeks |
| **MVP (Minimum Viable Product)** | Validate business viability | Only must-have features | 4–8 weeks |
| **MLP (Minimum Lovable Product)** | Delight early adopters | MVP + 1-2 polish features | 2–3 months |
| **V1 (First Release)** | Broader market readiness | MVP + polish, onboarding, stability | 3–6 months |
| **Full Product / V2+** | Scaling and monetization | Full features, enterprise readiness | 6+ months |

---

## **Key Notes**
- **MVP** is the smallest testable version we can ship to validate a product idea.
- **MLP** includes just enough polish to make it exciting for early adopters.
- Use this document as a **baseline reference** when discussing project scope.
